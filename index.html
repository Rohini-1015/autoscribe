🎙️ 100% Hands-Free Accessible Audio System

🎓 AutoScribe Autonomous Voice Exam Portal

Designed for 100% independent examination writing by blind and visually impaired students. Zero visual dependency. Simply listen to audio prompts and speak commands naturally to navigate, write, and submit your exam.

🎙️ Continuous Hands-Free Listening Active (Say "Login" or "Start Exam")

🔊 Click anywhere or press any key to activate Voice Assistant & Microphone

🗣️ Core Navigation Voice Commands

Speak clearly into your microphone
"Login"
Opens Student Voice Login Screen
"Start Exam"
Directly launches active examination session
"Read question"
Reads question statement aloud via Text-to-Speech
"Start answer"
Activates continuous voice dictation in Write Mode
"Read my answer"
Verifies transcribed text by reading back answer
"Submit exam"
Triggers secure submission with voice confirmation
